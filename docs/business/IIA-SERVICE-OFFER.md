# IIA Business Knowledge Assistant — Service Offer

**Status:** proposed commercial offer; do not advertise as production-ready until the acceptance checklist is passed.

## Positioning

IIA helps small businesses answer repetitive customer and staff questions using approved business information. The initial product is a managed, website-based knowledge assistant—not a promise of a fully autonomous agent.

## Ideal first customers

Start with one niche at a time:
1. Training institutes and education consultancies.
2. Small service businesses with recurring FAQs.
3. Small online shops with product and policy questions.

Qualify a prospect if they receive repeated questions, have source documents/FAQs, have a person responsible for approving answers, and can measure response time or enquiry volume.

## Pilot package

**Suggested test price:** NPR 8,000–12,000 one-time.

Includes:
- One narrow use case and one website/demo interface.
- Up to 20 approved FAQ entries or a small, agreed set of text-based documents.
- Basic answer testing and a defined fallback/human handoff.
- One review round and a short handover.
- A 14-day pilot window after acceptance.

Excludes unless separately agreed:
- Unlimited usage, OCR for scanned files, custom CRM/payment integrations, multilingual guarantees, 24/7 support, data migration, and new features.
- Third-party model, hosting, messaging, and payment fees beyond the agreed allowance.

## Managed business package

**Suggested test price:** NPR 15,000–25,000 setup plus NPR 3,000–6,000/month.

May include:
- Approved content ingestion and website embedding.
- A defined monthly usage allowance.
- Basic usage/error reporting.
- One scheduled content update per month.
- Business-hours support with a documented response target.

State usage limits, support hours, data retention, service dependencies, and overage pricing in every quote.

## Custom workflow

**Suggested starting quote:** NPR 30,000–75,000+, scoped after discovery.

Use for additional data sources, lead qualification, internal document workflows, or integrations. Quote separately for implementation, maintenance, and third-party usage.

## Acceptance criteria for each client

Before launch, agree on:
- The exact questions the assistant is expected to answer.
- Which approved sources it may use.
- Expected behavior when the source does not contain an answer.
- Data owner, permitted data, retention/deletion process, and access rules.
- Test questions and minimum acceptable results.
- Usage allowance, service hours, fees, and cancellation terms.

## Honest claims

Do not promise guaranteed sales increases, perfect accuracy, autonomous actions, enterprise security, or production reliability without evidence. Label a demo as a demo. Obtain permission before using customer content, logo, testimonial, or results in marketing.
