# IIA — Client Outreach Templates

Personalize every message. Do not claim a business has a problem until you have verified it. Do not send bulk spam.

## First message (email or LinkedIn)

**Subject:** Quick idea for handling repeated customer questions at [Business]

Hi [Name],

I’m building IIA, an AI knowledge assistant for businesses that answers a defined set of customer questions using approved FAQs and business documents, and hands off when it cannot find a reliable answer.

I noticed [specific, factual observation about the business]. Would reducing the time spent answering [relevant type of question] be useful for your team?

I can show you a short demo using sample information. If it looks relevant, we can discuss a small, paid pilot with a fixed scope.

Would a 15-minute conversation next week be useful?

Regards,  
Siddhartha  
Integrated Intelligence Agent (IIA)

## Short follow-up

Hi [Name], just following up on my note about a small AI assistant for [specific use case]. I can show a short demo using sample data, without needing access to your customer information. If it is not a priority right now, no problem.

## Discovery call questions

1. Which questions do customers or staff repeat most often?
2. How do you answer them today, and how much staff time does it take?
3. Where is the approved information stored (website, FAQs, PDFs, policy documents)?
4. What must the assistant never answer or disclose?
5. When should it hand a question to a person?
6. What result would make a pilot worth paying for?
7. Who approves the content, budget, and launch?
8. What privacy, retention, or compliance requirements apply?

## Demo structure (10 minutes)

1. Confirm the use case and expected outcome (1 minute).
2. Ask 3–5 questions covered by the approved sample content (3 minutes).
3. Show a question with no supported answer and demonstrate fallback/handoff (2 minutes).
4. Explain the content update, usage limits, data handling, and human oversight (2 minutes).
5. Confirm next steps and a fixed-scope paid pilot (2 minutes).

## Pilot proposal outline

- Customer and business problem.
- Scope and out-of-scope items.
- Approved sources and data owner.
- Deliverables and timeline.
- Test questions and acceptance criteria.
- Fees, payment schedule, usage limits, and third-party costs.
- Support hours, maintenance, and change-request fees.
- Privacy, access, retention/deletion, and incident contact.
- Customer responsibilities and approval/sign-off.

## Objection: “Can’t we just use a free chatbot?”

That may be the right choice if a generic tool already meets your needs. IIA’s value is the setup around your approved information, the agreed answer tests, website integration, fallback behavior, and ongoing maintenance. We should only proceed if that saves enough time or improves the enquiry experience to justify the cost.

## Objection: “Can you guarantee every answer is correct?”

No AI assistant can responsibly guarantee that. The pilot is designed to measure performance on agreed questions, cite approved information where supported, and fall back to a person when evidence is missing. High-risk decisions remain with qualified staff.
